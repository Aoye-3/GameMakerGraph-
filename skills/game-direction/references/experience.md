# Experience Lens

Use this lens for what the player perceives, understands, controls, and feels in one relevant journey slice.

## Follow the player moment

Consider entry, learning the goal and controls, reading state under real play, acting and receiving feedback,
failure/recovery, and completion. Do not audit every screen when the question concerns one moment.

## Evaluation axes

- **Goal clarity:** Can the player tell what matters now without an external explanation?
- **Affordance and mapping:** Do controls and interactive objects suggest what they do?
- **Information priority:** Does urgent information remain visible under motion, pressure, and effects?
- **Feedback coherence:** Do visual, audio, motion, and state feedback agree about cause and severity?
- **Onboarding:** Is each concept introduced near the moment it becomes useful, with a safe chance to apply it?
- **Friction:** How many steps, waits, or context switches separate intent from action?
- **Accessibility:** Does the critical interaction avoid relying only on color, tiny text, audio, precision, or one input?
- **Recovery:** After confusion or failure, is the next useful action clear?
- **Aesthetic coherence:** Do art, audio, UI, writing, and motion reinforce rather than obscure the player fantasy?

## Output

Anchor each finding to a player moment: when X occurs, the player lacks Y signal, so Z interpretation is
plausible. Recommend the smallest observable experiment. Useful signals include time to first successful
action, repeated wrong input, missed state changes, hesitation, and recovery time. Leave UI code and final
art production to their relevant workflows.
