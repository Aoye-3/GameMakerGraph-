# Gameplay Lens

Use this lens for the activity the player repeatedly performs, the decisions inside it, and the systems
that shape mastery over time.

## Start from the intended experience

Identify only what the current decision needs:

- the player fantasy or emotion being promised;
- the primary verb and shortest repeatable loop;
- the observable state change showing that the action mattered;
- the reason to repeat the loop after the first success.

Work backward from experience to dynamics and rules. Do not add conventional genre systems unless they
serve the intended loop.

## Evaluation axes

- **Action quality:** Is the primary action legible, responsive, and satisfying before progression rewards?
- **Meaningful decisions:** Do viable options produce different consequences, costs, or risks?
- **Feedback:** Can the player understand what happened, why, and what to try next?
- **Challenge and mastery:** Does challenge expose learnable patterns instead of requiring guesswork?
- **Failure and recovery:** Is failure cost proportional, and can the player quickly re-enter the decision?
- **Loop connection:** Do moment-to-moment actions support short goals, sessions, and progression?
- **System interaction:** Do mechanics create tradeoffs without collapsing into a dominant strategy?
- **Tuning leverage:** Which small set of variables materially changes feel, pacing, or strategy?

## Output

Return the experience to preserve, the highest-risk assumption, one rule/interaction/feedback change worth
testing, and an observable playtest signal. Establish intended behavior and a safe tuning range before
proposing exact formulas or implementation architecture.
